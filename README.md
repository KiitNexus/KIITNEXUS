## 🧭 Who We Are

**KIIT Nexus** is the official technical society of **KIIT University** — a community of students who learn by building, grow by collaborating, and turn ideas into working software. We exist for one reason: to help students go from _curious_ to _capable_.

We're not here to just talk about technology. We're here to ship it.

<br/>
